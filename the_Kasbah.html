1. Place of the martyrs

The Place of the Martyrs is a historic square in Algiers, Algeria. It is located in the heart of the city and is known for its significance in the country's history. The square is named in honor of those who sacrificed their lives for the cause of Algerian independence. It serves as a symbol of national pride and remembrance, and it is often used for public gatherings, ceremonies, and events that commemorate Algeria's struggle for freedom.

2. Dar Aziza Palace

Dar Aziza Palace is a historic palace located in the heart of Algiers, Algeria. It was built in the 19th century and served as the residence of the Bey of Algiers. The palace is known for its beautiful architecture and historical significance. It has been converted into a museum and is now open to the public for tours.

3. The Kaschou Mosque

The Kaschou Mosque is a historic mosque located in the heart of the Kasbah of Algiers. It is known for its beautiful architecture and historical significance.

4. National Museum of Arts and Popular Traditions

This lovely museum will show you lots of traditional clothes from all over Algeria and many old crafts of Algerian art, including rugs, jewellery and pottery.
