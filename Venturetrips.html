Algiers - the capital of Algeria

Kasbah of Algiers

Algiers is the capital and largest city of Algeria, located along the Mediterranean coast. It is a vibrant hub of culture, history, and commerce, offering visitors a glimpse into the heart of the country. Algiers is a huge city, with a number of interesting places to visit, such as -the Kasbah. Heart of the city with over two thousand years of history stands on the steep hill. The Kasbah is made of narrow lanes and beautiful architecture mostly from Ottoman Empire times.
